About the blog

A little corner, made with care.

Cha.WoominWorld is a personal, fan-made blog created from Nigeria to celebrate Cha Woo-min's acting journey and share thoughtful appreciation with other fans.

The story

Why I started Cha.WoominWorld

I wanted a small place on the internet where I could enjoy being a fan without making everything about chasing attention. Cha.WoominWorld is my way of putting that admiration into something creative: writing, organizing an archive, discovering projects and sharing what I enjoy about his performances.

I'm a fan writing from Nigeria, while Cha Woo-min is based in South Korea. That distance is part of what makes a fan archive meaningful to me: people can be in completely different places and still connect through stories, films and television.

What this blog will share

You'll find actor profiles, project guides, scene and character appreciation, personal reflections and occasional fan-made features. The goal is to keep the tone warm, respectful and genuinely interested in the work.

A small promise

Cha.WoominWorld will never claim to speak for Cha Woo-min, his agency or his private life. It is simply a fan space. Publicly available facts will be treated carefully, rumors will not be presented as facts, and personal information will stay private.

Fan disclaimer: Cha.WoominWorld is independent and is not officially affiliated with Cha Woo-min, his agency, or any production company.
